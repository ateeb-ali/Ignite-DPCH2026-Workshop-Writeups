# Digital Pakistan Cyber Hackathon 2026 — CTF Writeups

A collection of my **selected CTF (Capture The Flag) writeups** from the **Digital Pakistan Cyber Hackathon 2026** workshop organized by **Ignite – National Technology Fund** alongside **Trillium Information Security Systems** at the **NED University of Engineering & Technology** from 5th to 7th of September, 2026.

During the workshop, we worked through a variety of cybersecurity challenges and solved CTFs covering different areas of security. This repository documents some of the challenges I found interesting or worth revisiting, along with my approach and methodology for solving them.

> **Note:** This repository is **not a complete collection of all challenges** from the workshop. Only selected challenges are included.

---

### 🌐 Why aren't Web challenges included?

Web-based challenges from the workshop are intentionally **not included** in this repository.

Many of them relied on live challenge instances, temporary environments, or dynamically generated targets. Because of this, simply documenting the solution may not provide a reproducible experience for someone reading the writeup later.

Therefore, this repository focuses primarily on challenges whose solutions can be reasonably documented and reproduced without access to the original live instances.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

This repository serves as:

* A personal record of my progress during the workshop
* A reference for revisiting techniques I learned
* A learning resource for others interested in CTFs
* Documentation of selected cybersecurity challenges I found interesting

More writeups may be added over time.
